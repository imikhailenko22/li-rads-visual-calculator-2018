What is the LI-RADS Visual Calculator?

This free, independent educational calculator helps radiologists and trainees apply the published CT/MRI LI-RADS v2018 diagnostic framework. Users can select modality, observation features and size, review supporting imaging examples and pitfalls, examine major and ancillary findings, and generate draft structured reporting language.

LI-RADS (Liver Imaging Reporting and Data System) is maintained by the American College of Radiology (ACR). This project is independently developed and is not an official ACR product.

Key features

Guided v2018 categorization

Enter observation details and major features under the published v2018 CT/MRI criteria.

Visual imaging references

Review example images and selected pitfalls for APHE, washout, capsule and other features.

Ancillary feature assessment

Consider applicable ancillary findings within the v2018 framework.

Structured reports

Generate editable draft report wording and review the result against official criteria.

How to use the calculator

  1. Confirm the patient and examination meet the applicable LI-RADS population and technical requirements.
  2. Select the imaging modality and enter the observation findings requested by the calculator.
  3. Use the visual references when reviewing major, ancillary, or special imaging features.
  4. Review the resulting category and generated reporting text against the official algorithm before using it.
